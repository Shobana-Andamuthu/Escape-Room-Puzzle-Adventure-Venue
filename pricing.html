Frequently Asked Questions & Policies

Everything you need to know regarding private room bookings, headcount adjustments, rescheduling rules, and arrival briefings.

Every single booking at Vault & Key is strictly private. Once you reserve your preferred time slot, that chamber is locked down solely for your team. You will never be grouped with guests outside your party.

You can easily add additional players upon arrival up to the maximum capacity of your selected chamber. Extra players simply settle their ticket at check-in. If your group decreases, we adjust your tier rate accordingly.

You can reschedule your mission with zero penalty fees up to 24 hours prior to launch. If you need to cancel, we provide a non-expiring venue voucher equal to your full payment value for future booking.

Please arrive promptly 15 minutes before your scheduled start time. This ensures ample time for secure locker storage, waiver sign-offs, and your cinematic mission briefing from the Game Master.

Our chambers are tailored for players aged 10 and older. Participants aged 14 and under must be accompanied inside the chamber by at least one paying adult guardian. We also offer young-solver clue modes for youth parties.

No heavy physical exertion or dangerous stunts are involved. Our challenges focus on observation, deduction, communication, and mechanical curiosity. Every chamber features an illuminated safety override push-button for immediate exit if needed.
