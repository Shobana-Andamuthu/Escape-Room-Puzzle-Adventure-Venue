Visitor Arrival & Practical FAQs

Helpful guidance to ensure your squad enjoys a seamless, punctual, and comfortable mission experience.

We offer complimentary validated parking at the Metro Vault Garage directly opposite our entrance on Cipher Street. Simply bring your parking ticket to the front reception desk for validation upon check-in.

Yes. Complimentary electronic passcode lockers are provided in our mission preparation lounge. All coats, backpacks, and personal electronic devices can be safely locked prior to chamber entry.

Food and beverages are permitted inside our private VIP Debrief Lounges for birthday and corporate bookings. To protect electronic props and delicate mechanisms, food and drinks are strictly prohibited inside the escape chambers.

Comfortable casual wear and closed-toe flat shoes are ideal. You may need to kneel, reach into compartments, or examine clues under tables, but no vigorous athletics or climbing are required.

Please call our dispatch desk immediately. To ensure that teams scheduled after yours are not delayed, mission clocks must begin on schedule. However, late-arriving members may join your chamber once briefed by the Game Master.

Yes. Our facility features street-level step-free access, wide corridors, and accessible restrooms. Chambers including The Obsidian Vault and Cyberpunk 2088 are designed with wheelchair-friendly navigation and reachable puzzle consoles.
